Privacy Policy

How this personal portfolio handles information.

Information collected

This portfolio does not have a contact form, account registration, or newsletter. If you contact Jakkapan Makim by email or telephone, the information you choose to provide will be used to respond to you and handle your inquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

Site preferences

This website stores your language and appearance preferences (English or Thai, and light or dark mode) in your browser's local storage. These settings stay in your browser and are not sent to the site owner by this website.

Hosting and third-party services

This website is intended to be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as connection data and server logs, to provide and secure its services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details. The site does not currently use analytics, advertising, embedded social media, or a contact form. If these services change, this policy should be updated.

Retention and your requests

Messages sent directly to the site owner are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain related records, unless a longer period is required by law. To ask about, access, correct, or request deletion of information you sent, contact J4ckpot.bank@gmail.com. The site owner will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page. Questions can be sent to J4ckpot.bank@gmail.com.
